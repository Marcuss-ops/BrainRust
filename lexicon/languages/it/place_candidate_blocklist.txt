# Two families live here:

#   2. institutions and offices that are never a map stop ("corte",
#      "cassazione", "procura", "tribunale").
# Given names and surnames whose homonymous settlements hijacked real places
# live in place_candidate_token_blocklist.txt, because they disqualify a whole
